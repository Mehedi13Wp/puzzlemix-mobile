PuzzleMix Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

PuzzleMix is a casual puzzle game developed and published by Mehedi Ahamed.

Data collection

PuzzleMix does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. The current version does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, account systems, location services, contacts, camera, microphone, or other sensitive device permissions.

Local game data

Game progress such as coins, stars, completed levels, tutorial status, and sound preferences is stored locally on your device and is not sent to the developer.

Audio

The game plays bundled sound effects and background music from local app assets. Audio playback does not require microphone or media-library access.

Children and families

PuzzleMix is a general-audience puzzle game. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children or other users.

Retention and deletion

Because PuzzleMix has no developer-controlled account or cloud database in the current release, local data can be deleted by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Changes

If future versions add ads, analytics, cloud saves, accounts, or online services, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before release.

Contact

Support and privacy inquiries: PuzzleMix support channel.
