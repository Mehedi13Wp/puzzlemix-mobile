# In-game fruit artwork

Built-in image generation tool produced `assets/images/fruit-atlas.png`.
The transparent atlas uses four columns and two rows. Reading order: banana,
orange, mango, apple, grapes, strawberry, kiwi, jackfruit. Fruit IDs and game
rules are unchanged. These are rendered sprites, not realtime 3D meshes.

Prompt: Eight individually centered fruits in a regular 4 by 2 transparent
sprite atlas, glossy sculpted 3D game style, studio lighting from upper left,
rich colors and clear silhouettes at small sizes. Top row: banana, orange,
mango, apple. Bottom row: grapes, strawberry, sliced kiwi, jackfruit.
No text, frames, background, floor or overlapping sprites.
