## Full description

PuzzleMix is a polished casual puzzle collection designed for quick, relaxing play.

Start with Fruit Sort Deluxe, where beautiful fruit pieces are moved between premium glass jars until every jar contains a perfect match. Enjoy the deep green garden atmosphere, smooth movement, satisfying feedback, and rewarding level celebrations.

When you want a different challenge, switch to Arrow Escape and clear arrows in the correct order, or play Color Crew and solve colorful matching boards.

FEATURES

• Fruit Sort Deluxe with polished fruit artwork and glass jars  
• Deep green premium visual style  
• Smooth animations, satisfying sound effects, and haptic feedback  
• Daily Harvest challenge with bonus rewards  
• Multiple puzzle modes in one lightweight game  
• Hint, undo, restart, and extra-jar helpers  
• Stars, coins, level progression, and celebrations  
• No account required  
• No ads in the current release  
• Game progress stored locally on your device

PuzzleMix is built for short breaks, calm play sessions, and anyone who enjoys satisfying sorting and logic puzzles.

Relax. Match. Enjoy.
